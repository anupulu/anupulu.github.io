Participated in herHACK 2024, Switzerland's largest female-led hackathon, where our team secured 2nd place.

## The Challenge

We tackled the challenge of how might we raise awareness among the SBB developer community in green and sustainable coding practices. The goal was to create solutions that would:

- Raise awareness about green coding among developers and management
- Bring sustainability to the forefront of software devopment practices
- Introduce concrete measures or programs to guide sustainable development in everyday activities

## Our Solution

We developed a prototype of:

1. **Script for evaluating how sustainable the code is**: Runs through the code and gives actionable feedback
2. **New section on their existing dashboard showing green coding metrics**: Shows relevant metrics that can be looked at in weekly meetings
3. **Grass roots ownership**: Assigning ambassadors among developers to keep up to date and share green coding guidelines at SBB

## Key Learnings

1. **Rapid Prototyping**: The importance of quick iterations and feedback in hackathon settings
2. **Clear Communication**: The value of presenting technical solutions to non-technical audiences
3. **Team Collaboration**: Effective coordination in time-constrained environments

## Impact & Recognition

Our solution was recognized for its:
- Approach to integrate it in existing workflows
- Taking into consideration different roles (e.g. developers and managers)
