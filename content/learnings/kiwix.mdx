Contributing to Kiwix, an open-source project that makes Wikipedia and other educational content available offline, reaching millions of users without reliable internet access.

## The Impact

Kiwix enables access to knowledge for:
- Students in areas with limited connectivity
- Educational institutions in developing regions
- Communities during humanitarian crises
- Inhabitants in countries where the internet is censored
- Users with bandwidth constraints

## My Contributions

### General Improvements
- Email automation scenarios (newsletters + fundraising emails)
- Visual and content improvements in app stores and GitHub repositories & readme files
- User Survey creation

## Key Learnings

1. **Global Impact**: Open source contributions can reach millions of users worldwide
2. **Digital Inclusion**: Technical solutions can bridge significant digital divides
3. **Community Power**: Collaborative development drives meaningful change
4. **User-Centric Design**: Importance of considering diverse user needs and constraints
