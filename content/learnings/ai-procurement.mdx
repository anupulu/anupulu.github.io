Built an AI and blockchain-powered platform for transparent and efficient public procurement processes during the AI for Connectivity Hackathon. The solution aimed to reduce corruption and improve resource allocation in public sector procurement.

## Key Features

- **AI-assisted RFP creation**: AI-assisted creation of RFPs
- **Automated Bid Evaluation**: AI-powered analysis and evaluation of procurement proposals
- **Transparent Audit Trails**: Blockchain-based immutable record keeping

## Key Learnings

1. **Technology Integration**: Combining AI and blockchain technologies can create more transparent procurement processes
2. **Balance**: Public sector innovation requires careful balance of efficiency and accountability
3. **Team Dynamics**: Cross-functional teams and skillsets enable comprehensive problem-solving in complex domains

## Impact & Next Steps

The platform demonstrated how emerging technologies can be leveraged to serve public good while maintaining accountability. We're currently exploring opportunities to:

- Refine the solution: focusing on core features and real-life procurement scenarios
- Open-source strategy: Leaning more towards open source & establishing our strategy
- Considering feedback from mentors and improving our solution
